Privacy Policy

This policy explains what personal information Decent Image Media collects through this website, why we collect it, and the choices you have. We have kept it short and plain.

Who we are

Decent Image Media is a branding, print, signage and exhibitions company based in Kenya. We are responsible for the personal information described in this policy. You can reach us on WhatsApp at +254 182 832454.

What we collect, and when

When you send the enquiry form

The form asks for your name, email address, business name, industry and the service you are interested in. You can also add a phone number and a link to your website or Instagram page. When you press send, these details are passed through FormSubmit, a form delivery service, and arrive as an email in our consultancy inbox.

When you use the chat assistant

The assistant on this site answers from a fixed list of answers that runs inside your own browser. What you type is not sent to us and is not stored. If you tap "Continue on WhatsApp", your question is written into a WhatsApp message, and we receive it only if you send that message.

When you message or call us directly

We receive whatever you choose to share, along with your phone number or email address, so that we can reply.

What we do not collect

We do not ask for payment details on this website, and we cannot see the names or contact details of people who browse without contacting us.

Cookies and similar technologies

When you first visit, we ask whether you accept optional cookies. Until you choose "Accept all", none of the optional tools below run.

Essential (always on)

  • A note that remembers your cookie choice, so we do not ask again.
  • A note that stops the welcome greeting from repeating during a visit. It is deleted when you close the tab.

Analytics (only if you accept)

We use Google Analytics to count visits and see which pages are useful, such as how many people view our exhibition work. Google Analytics uses cookies and receives your IP address, device and browser details. We use this information in summary form, not to identify you.

Advertising (only if you accept)

We use the Meta Pixel (Facebook and Instagram) and Google Ads tags to measure whether our ads work and to show our ads again to people who have visited this site. These companies may connect your visit with your account on their platforms, under their own privacy policies. We see only grouped results, such as how many visitors sent an enquiry. We do not receive your name or contact details from them.

Changing your choice

Use the "Cookie settings" link at the bottom of our home page at any time. You can also clear cookies in your browser settings. You can control ad preferences in your Google and Meta account settings.

How we use your information

  • To reply to your enquiry and answer your questions.
  • To prepare a quote, proposal or free brand audit you have asked for.
  • If you accept optional cookies, to understand how the site is used and to measure and improve our advertising.
  • To deliver and install work you have ordered, and to keep the records the law requires, such as invoices.

We do not sell your information, and we do not add you to marketing lists without your agreement.

Our legal basis

We handle personal information under Kenya's Data Protection Act, 2019. We rely on your consent when you choose to contact us, on the steps needed to prepare or carry out a contract with you, and on our legitimate interest in running and improving our business.

Who else is involved

  • FormSubmit: delivers the enquiry form to our email inbox.
  • Our email provider: stores the emails we receive.
  • WhatsApp (Meta): handles messages you send us on WhatsApp, under its own privacy policy.
  • Google (Analytics and Ads) and Meta (Facebook and Instagram): only if you accept optional cookies, as described above.
  • Google Fonts: the typefaces on this site load from Google's servers, so Google receives your device's IP address when a page loads.
  • Our website host: like all hosting companies, ours keeps standard technical logs, such as IP addresses and pages requested, to keep the site secure and running.
  • Production and delivery partners: when you place an order, we share only what a supplier or installer needs to complete it, such as a delivery location.

Some of these companies store information outside Kenya.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry messages for as long as we need them to respond and follow up. If you become a client, we keep project and billing records for the length of our work together and for the period required by tax and business law. You can ask us to delete your enquiry at any time.

Your rights

Under the Data Protection Act you have the right to:

  • be told how we use your information;
  • ask for a copy of the information we hold about you;
  • ask us to correct information that is wrong or out of date;
  • ask us to delete your information, or to stop using it;
  • withdraw your consent at any time.

To use any of these rights, message us on WhatsApp at +254 182 832454. If you are not satisfied with our response, you can complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner of Kenya.

Photos of our work

This site shows photographs of stands, signage and events we have delivered. Some include people who were present. If you appear in a photo and would like it removed, tell us and we will take it down.

Children

This website is for businesses and is not aimed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle personal information, we will update this page and the date at the top before the change takes effect.
